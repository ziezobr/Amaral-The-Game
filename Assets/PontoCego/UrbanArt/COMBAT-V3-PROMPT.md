## Prompt final

Generate a CLEAN GAME-READY sprite sheet, not concept art. 4 columns × 8 rows, exactly 32 isolated full-body Amaral chibi sprites. Match the approved character in the two references: curly brown hair, dark glasses, short beard, charcoal T-shirt, beige cargo trousers, gray-black sneakers, black camera and bag. SAME close-up overhead topdown chibi viewpoint and painted shading as reference 2. Each row contains 4 distinct consecutive unarmed attack poses (guard, windup, punch or elbow extension, recovery), camera stays on his strap safely at his chest. Top-to-bottom facing directions south, southwest, west, northwest, north, northeast, east, southeast. Keep each character fully inside its cell, centered foot pivot, identical body size, ample empty transparent gaps. Critical art rule: ONLY clean DARK BLACK ink for EVERY outer silhouette and ALL contours. Absolutely NO RED anywhere in the image: no red strap, no red outline, no red rimlight, no red shadow, no red fringe or red marks. Camera strap is BLACK. No gold aura, no colored aura, no motion trail, no debris, no sparkle, no marks outside silhouettes. Skin warm peach, hair brown, costume charcoal/beige/gray; all outlining clean black. Draw the 32 sprites freshly with crisp smooth clean alpha contours, no matting colors and no random edge pixels. Genuine transparent RGBA background, no floor shadows, no ground, no panels, no labels, no text, no border. All artwork must be finished sprite illustration usable in Unity without manual cleanup. Wide transparent gutters separate all 32 cells. New combat poses in the existing approved style; do not reproduce the walking poses.
